# 05 | Candidate materials and manufacturing

**Status:** Initial engineering research questions; not approved clinical choices.

## Candidate 1: Ti-6Al-4V ELI
- Established implant-grade titanium alloy family, high stiffness and strength relative to mass.
- Candidate fabrication: laser powder bed fusion or appropriately validated machining, with downstream processing and inspection.
- Investigate: implant standards, fatigue, anisotropy, surface roughness, imaging artefacts, thermal conduction, cleaning and sterilisation.

## Candidate 2: Implant-grade PEEK
- Polyetheretherketone, a high-performance thermoplastic; lower modulus and density than titanium and generally less imaging artefact.
- Candidate fabrication: CNC machining from certified stock or suitably validated high-temperature additive manufacturing.
- Investigate: grade/certification, time-dependent creep, fixation design, fatigue, sterilisation, manufacturing quality and material traceability.

## Typical starting comparison (not validated design values)
| Attribute | Ti-6Al-4V ELI | PEEK |
|---|---|---|
| Young's modulus | ~110 GPa | ~3–4 GPa |
| Density | ~4.43 g/cm³ | ~1.3 g/cm³ |
| Thermal conductivity | ~6–7 W/m·K | ~0.25 W/m·K |
| Trade-off | High stiffness; metal AM lattices | Lower stiffness; potential larger inward deflection |

**No PMMA concept is pursued in the present comparison** at the designer's request.

## Manufacturing notes
- Metal patient-specific lattices may be made by powder bed fusion, followed by process validation and finishing.
- Unique polymer implants can be CNC-machined from implant-grade PEEK stock.
- 'CNC is always more precise than moulding' is too broad: accuracy depends on process qualification, geometry, tooling, tolerances and inspection.
- Prototype PLA/PETG parts made on a Bambu A1 Mini are **non-clinical visual/fit demonstration parts**, not implant material verification.

## Evidence to collect
Manufacturer and standard-specific material data, design allowables, defect/screw constraints, manufacturable dimensions, qualification evidence and peer-reviewed comparative studies.
