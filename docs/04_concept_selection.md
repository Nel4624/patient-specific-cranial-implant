## Draft weighted objectives
| Criterion | Weight (%) |
|---|---:|
| Tissue protection and inward displacement | 25 |
| Bone load distribution and stresses | 20 |
| Anatomical fit, contour and patient satisfaction | 15 |
| Fixation stability | 15 |
| Biocompatibility/long-term suitability | 10 |
| Surgical access | 5 |
| Manufacturability and quality assurance | 5 |
| Imaging and thermal considerations | 3 |
| Implant mass | 2 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Important:** Critical safety and feasibility requirements are hard gates, not tradeable scores. These weights are user-proposed priorities, not validated clinical stakeholder weights.

## Scoring protocol
- Define evidence-backed rubrics for scores 1–5 for each criterion.
- Record justification, uncertainty and evidence reference for every score.
- Weighted result: sum(weight_i × score_i / 5).
- Compare sensitivity to weights and alternative clinical priorities.
- Do not assign confident rankings from aesthetics or intuition alone.
