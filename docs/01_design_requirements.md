# 01 | Stakeholders and design requirements

**Status:** Working draft, not verified clinical specifications.

## Primary objective
Develop and comparatively assess a patient-specific cranial reconstruction concept that prioritises patient safety, restoration of contour, stable fixation, recovery, satisfaction and long-term quality of life.

**Acceptance values intentionally blank:** Patient-specific bone quality, defect dimensions, clinically justified loads, screw specifications, tissue clearances and failure criteria have not been provided/verified.

## Clinical boundary
Cerebrospinal fluid containment depends on intact meningeal structures (especially the dura/arachnoid), not a hermetic skull implant. A compressible perimeter gasket is a rejected exploratory concept pending any evidence-based indication. Infection/CSF-leak risk still warrants discussion and surgical review.

## Research questions
- Is an overlapping peripheral lip beneficial compared with discrete fixation tabs after accounting for local contact/bone stress?
- How do titanium alloy and implant-grade PEEK compare across displacement, stress, mass and image compatibility?
- How can clinical contour and contact accuracy be maintained through simplification and CAD reconstruction?
