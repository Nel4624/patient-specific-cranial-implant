# 03 | Concept pros and cons

These are **hypotheses for analysis**, not claims of verified performance.

| Concept | Potential advantages | Potential drawbacks and investigations |
|---|---|---|
| A Solid + continuous lip | Straightforward baseline; continuous contact area | Larger mass; high stiffness; contact stress and contour prominence |
| B Perforated + continuous lip | Lower mass; possible tissue-related benefits depending on context; peripheral contact | Stress concentrations around perforations; fatigue; smooth finishing |
| C Hex lattice + continuous lip | Lightweight; compatible with metal AM; shell stiffness tailoring | Feature validation; lattice fatigue; cleaning/inspection; FEA complexity |
| D Graded lattice + continuous lip | Potential site-specific stiffness/load tailoring | Many variables; manufacturing, validation and numerical complexity |
| E Flush + tabs | Smaller contact footprint; possible low profile | Load concentrated at tabs and screws; inward displacement risk |
| F Radial reinforcement + lip | Potentially stronger load paths toward boundary | Stiffness transitions; possible local contact stress; complexity |

## Rejected/parked concept
A compressible gasket was considered to improve fluid sealing, but skull-implant sealing is not the principal mechanism of CSF containment; the meninges maintain the relevant barrier. The gasket would introduce biocompatibility, movement, creep, degradation and infection questions. Do not include one without a demonstrated indication and testing plan.
