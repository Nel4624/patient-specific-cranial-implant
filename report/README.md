# Engineering report workspace

Suggested final structure (adapt to the supplied report rubric):
1. Executive summary / design problem
2. Background and clinical context
3. Stakeholder needs and engineering requirements
4. Anatomy and input mesh analysis
5. Morphological table and concept generation
6. Pro/con analysis and weighted selection
7. Material and manufacturing assessment
8. CAD reconstruction and geometric validation
9. FEA setup, convergence and comparative results
10. Final concept and prototyping
11. Discussion: assumptions, limitations, patient safety and future verification
12. Conclusion and references

**Reporting rule:** Distinguish planned, simulated and physically tested work. Cite independently verified sources, preserve traceability, and meet any competition/university disclosure rules.
